How We Get There

Three pillars carry our 2030 and 2040 commitments forward.

Water & Waste

We're working toward net positive water use and zero waste to landfill by 2030 — conserving tens of billions of gallons and returning more freshwater to local communities than our operations consume.

Learn more about water & waste

Climate Commitment

In 2023, Intel pledged net-zero Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 2040, backed by a Climate Transition Action Plan spanning our global manufacturing footprint.

Learn more about our climate pledge

RISE Strategy

Our RISE strategy — Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling — sets ambitious 2030 goals that guide how our technology and our people drive progress on climate, water, and waste.
